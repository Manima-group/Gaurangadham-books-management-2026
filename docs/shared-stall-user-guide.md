# Shared Book Stall — User Guide

**Menu → Shared Book Stall.**

1. **Create** — give a name, date, place; choose *Only my Dham* or *Several Dhams*. You become the coordinator.
2. **Invite** — pick devotees of your Dham, or (several Dhams) type their mobile number. They tap **Accept & Join**.
3. **Add my books** — each person enters how many of their own books they bring. They stay yours; they are only kept aside for the stall.
4. **Open Stall — Start Selling** (coordinator).
5. **Quick Sell** — tap + on each book, choose Cash / UPI / Both, choose who received the money, **Record Sale**. Made a mistake? **Undo** (with a reason) — it is recorded, not deleted.
6. **Stop Selling — Start Closing** (coordinator), then **count the books left** on the table. If copies are missing, write a short note.
7. The app suggests what each person takes home (in proportion to what they gave). The coordinator can change it. Each owner taps **I confirm** or **Something is wrong**.
8. The Sector Sevak approves. Everyone's books are updated automatically.
9. **Hand over money** — whoever received money goes to Submit Money and chooses the stall. When the Sector Sevak marks it received, the stall closes.
